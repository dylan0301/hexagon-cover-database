# Strategy 2 Readability Diagnosis: Required Architecture

## 4. Required four-layer architecture

### Layer A: reader-facing geometry

The body should contain only:

- the geometric meaning of one transfer;
- the exact conditions licensing an identity relaxation;
- the active-gap kernel;
- the four possible terminal contradiction shapes;
- the branch-to-certificate routing table;
- the final branch closure proposition.

The body should not contain exact signed center equations, radical roots,
contact-cell formulas, or optimization domains.

### Layer B: geometry-to-parameter bridge

For each routed state, a bridge proposition must produce a named real record.
For example:

- endpoint record $(a_-,a_+,c_-,c_+)$;
- return record $(X,H,c_1,\ldots,c_5)$;
- rescuer record $(b,c,u)$;
- adjacent or nonadjacent Vd radial record.

The bridge proposition may use geometry.  It must end with a statement of the
form

$$
\mathbf p\in D_{\mathsf{id}}.
$$

It must not prove the terminal optimization inequality.

### Layer C: pure optimization registry

For every identifier $\mathsf{id}$, state:

1. independent real variables;
2. all derived quantities;
3. the exact feasible domain;
4. the branch decomposition;
5. the objective function;
6. the strictness convention;
7. the required sign.

No geometric noun should occur inside items 1--7.

### Layer D: verification

The verification file proves the optimization statements.  It may use exact
algebra, monotonicity, resultants, Bernstein certificates, or proof-assistant
code.  It must not invoke an unlisted geometric fact.

## 5. Recommended Strategy 2 narrative

The reader-facing chapter should begin with the following sentence-level idea:

> An incoming boundary demand and a radial demand bound the outgoing reach of
> one V triangle.  Coverage turns that outgoing upper bound into the next
> incoming lower bound.  Repetition ends in a cycle, an endpoint deficit, a
> returned-demand excess, or radial separation.

Only after this idea is established should the paper introduce the gap-rank
table.

The all-Vd0 kernel should be displayed as

| actual count | $\mathrm{gr}=0$ | $\mathrm{gr}=1$ | $\mathrm{gr}=2$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $N_+=0$ | strict cycle | one-gap endpoints | paired endpoints |
| $N_+=1$ | nine-point obstruction | five-role return | paired endpoints |

This table explains nearly the whole Strategy 2 routing.  The zero-gap
$N_+=1$ cell should be explicitly assigned to Strategy 4 rather than
described through a transfer chain that is not used.
