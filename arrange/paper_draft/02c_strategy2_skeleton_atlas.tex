\subsection{Hexagon-skeleton atlas for the two interfaces}
\label{sec:interface-notation-atlas}

Every quantity in the two notation-heavy interfaces is placed on the regular
hexagon skeleton below.  Each panel is a separate TikZ source and a separate
figure, so it can be retained or removed independently.  A dashed extremal
segment is not an actual trace, and a panel marked schematic records incidence
and order rather than metric placement.  The figures explain definitions and
hypotheses; they do not replace the inequalities in
Propositions~\ref{prop:body-transfer-interface} and
\ref{prop:body-signed-center-interface}.
