\section{Trace-Length Bounds}
\label{sec:trace-length}

The first mechanism compares the total one-dimensional trace available from
the seven triangles with the length of either the perimeter $\partial H$ or
the full skeleton $S$.  The local trace estimates, the master perimeter
deficit, the conditional skeleton theorem, and every routed application are
proved in this section.
