\section{Boundary-Reach Propagation}
\label{sec:boundary-propagation}

The second mechanism converts a backward boundary reach and a radial reach
into an upper bound for the forward reach of the same V triangle.
Coverage of the next edge then produces a lower bound for the next backward
reach.  The zero-gap cases have already been dispatched to Methods~1, 3, and
4 in Table~\ref{tab:routing}; every reader-facing application of Method~2 in
this section therefore has $N_{\mathrm{gap}}\ge1$.  We first distinguish the
one- and two-gap chains and then refine by $N_+$, $(d,t)$, and center type.
The section develops the common equilateral-enclosure calculus, the
center-assisted path budget, the exceptional T3-like and Vd placements, and
the complete branchwise propagation proof.  Formalization-only optimization
registries are kept in the repository but are not repeated in the printed
article.
