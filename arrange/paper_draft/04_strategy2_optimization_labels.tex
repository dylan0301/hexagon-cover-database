\subsection{The four algebraic branches}

Let
\[
 \mathfrak B=\{\mathrm{Lin},\mathrm{Const},Q_-,Q_+\}.
\]
For \((c,x)\in(1/2,1)\times[0,1]\), define the following four pairwise
disjoint cells:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{\mathrm{Lin}}
 &=\{(c,x):x\le1-c\text{ or }c\le x\},\\
 \mathcal C_{\mathrm{Const}}
 &=\left\{(c,x):
 \frac12<c\le\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\ x=h(c)\right\}\\
 &\qquad{}\cup
 \left\{(c,x):
 \frac{\sqrt3}{2}<c<1,\ \ell(c)<x<r(c)\right\},\\
 \mathcal C_{Q_-}
 &=\left\{(c,x):
 \frac12<c\le\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\ h(c)<x<c\right\}\\
 &\qquad{}\cup
 \left\{(c,x):
 \frac{\sqrt3}{2}<c<1,\ r(c)\le x<c\right\},\\
 \mathcal C_{Q_+}
 &=\left\{(c,x):
 \frac12<c\le\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\ 1-c<x<h(c)\right\}\\
 &\qquad{}\cup
 \left\{(c,x):
 \frac{\sqrt3}{2}<c<1,\ 1-c<x\le\ell(c)\right\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Write
\[
 \mathrm{Cell}_{\beta}(c,x)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 (c,x)\in\mathcal C_\beta.
\]
The equality \(x=h(c)\) is assigned to the constant branch,
\(x=\ell(c)\) to the \(Q_+\) branch, and \(x=r(c)\) to the \(Q_-\) branch.
Hence
\[
 (1/2,1)\times[0,1]
 =
 \bigsqcup_{\beta\in\mathfrak B}\mathcal C_\beta.
\]
No algebraic expression is optimized outside its own cell.
