\subsection{Finite contact-label cells}

Let
\[
 \mathfrak L=\{\mathrm{Full},L,T_-,T_+\}.
\]
For \((c,x)\in(1/2,1)\times[0,1]\), define the following four pairwise
disjoint predicates:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_{\mathrm{Full}}
 &=\{(c,x):x\le1-c\text{ or }c\le x\},\\
 \mathcal C_L
 &=\left\{(c,x):
 \frac12<c\le\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\ x=h(c)\right\}\\
 &\qquad{}\cup
 \left\{(c,x):
 \frac{\sqrt3}{2}<c<1,\ L(c)<x<U(c)\right\},\\
 \mathcal C_{T_-}
 &=\left\{(c,x):
 \frac12<c\le\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\ h(c)<x<c\right\}\\
 &\qquad{}\cup
 \left\{(c,x):
 \frac{\sqrt3}{2}<c<1,\ U(c)\le x<c\right\},\\
 \mathcal C_{T_+}
 &=\left\{(c,x):
 \frac12<c\le\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\ 1-c<x<h(c)\right\}\\
 &\qquad{}\cup
 \left\{(c,x):
 \frac{\sqrt3}{2}<c<1,\ 1-c<x\le L(c)\right\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Write
\[
 \mathrm{Cell}_{\ell}(c,x)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 (c,x)\in\mathcal C_\ell.
\]
The equality \(x=h(c)\) is assigned to \(L\), \(x=L(c)\) is assigned to
\(T_+\), and \(x=U(c)\) is assigned to \(T_-\).  Hence
\[
 (1/2,1)\times[0,1]
 =
 \bigsqcup_{\ell\in\mathfrak L}\mathcal C_\ell.
\]
This is the finite branch index used by the endpoint problems.  No algebraic
expression is optimized outside its own cell.
