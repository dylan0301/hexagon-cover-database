\section{Strategy 2 as Real-Variable Optimization}
\label{sec:strategy2-optimization-problems}

This appendix is the formalization interface for the scalar part of
Strategy~2.  Every registered problem contains only:
\begin{enumerate}
\item finitely many real input variables;
\item explicit radical or rational derived functions;
\item a finite union of branchwise semialgebraic cells;
\item an objective or recurrence; and
\item a required strict or weak sign.
\end{enumerate}
No hexagon, triangle, boundary edge, role type, translated object, placement
coordinate, or supremum occurs in a problem definition.  The sole dictionary
back to geometry is Appendix~\ref{sec:strategy2-parameter-bridges}.
