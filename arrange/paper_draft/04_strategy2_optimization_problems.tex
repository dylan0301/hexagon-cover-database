\section{Finite Inequalities for Boundary-Reach Propagation}
\label{sec:strategy2-optimization-problems}

This appendix states the finite real-variable inequalities used by Method~2.
Each statement contains only finitely many real variables, explicit radical or
rational functions, a finite union of semialgebraic cells, and a required sign.
The geometric derivation of the variables is given in
Appendix~\ref{sec:strategy2-parameter-bridges}.
