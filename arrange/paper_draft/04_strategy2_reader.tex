actual-row interfaces.  Section~\ref{sec:universal-calculus} supplies the
canonical maps

 g_c,\qquad \widehat g_c,\qquad

\subsection{The all-\(\Vdzero\) gap-rank kernel}

Let $\mathrm{gr}$ be the number of positive center traces that contain a
V-gap.  Then
\[
 \mathrm{gr}\in\{0,1,2\},
 \qquad
 \mathrm{gr}\le1\quad\text{for }\CEone.
\]
The two all-$\Vdzero$ row counts are governed by
\begin{center}
\scriptsize
\begin{tabular}{c|ccc}
 &$\mathrm{gr}=0$&$\mathrm{gr}=1$&$\mathrm{gr}=2$\\ \hline
$N_+=0$&
strict identity cycle&
one-side endpoint chain&
paired endpoint chain\\
$N_+=1$&
nine-point obstruction&
five-row relaxed chain&
paired endpoint chain.
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The last column is CE2-only.

For $N_+=0$ and $\mathrm{gr}=0$, no center trace contains a missed handoff;
hence the six incident vertex traces alone cover every boundary edge.  Thus
\[
 A_{i+1}>1-B_i
 \ge1-\widehat g_{C_i}(1-A_i)
 =\widehat g_{C_i}^\vee(A_i)
 \ge A_i.
\]
Iteration gives the impossible cycle
\[
 A_0<A_1<\cdots<A_5<A_0.
\]

For $N_+=0$ and $\mathrm{gr}=1$, Proposition
\ref{prop:body-all-vd0-endpoints} retains the two endpoint caps exactly.  The
center trace lies on the endpoint edge, while the three intervening edges are
center-free; Lemma~\ref{lem:reader-boundary-path} therefore applies to the
three middle nonsupercritical rows.  The T3-like $N_+=0$ branch has the same
ordinary path after the endpoint components are replaced by the exact
residuals of Definition~\ref{def:body-t3-endpoint-state}.

\begin{proposition}[Common CE2 two-gap application]
\label{prop:reader-common-two-gap}
Assume both CE2 center traces contain V-gaps and rows $T_1,\ldots,T_5$ are
nonsupercritical Vd0 rows.  Then the perimeter is not covered.  No hypothesis
on the criticality of $T_0$ is required.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Put
\[
 p=W-\alpha,\qquad q=R-\delta.
\]
The two gaps force endpoint inputs $p,q$ and radial demands $p/W,q/R$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:body-all-vd0-endpoints} gives
\[
 B_5+B_1
 \le\widehat g_{p/W}(1-p)+\widehat g_{q/R}(1-q)<1.
\]
The center traces lie only on the two endpoint edges.  The three middle rows
$T_2,T_3,T_4$ therefore form a center-free path through the four intervening
edges.  Lemma~\ref{lem:reader-boundary-path} forces their total boundary
contribution above three, contrary to their three unit caps.
\end{proof}

For $N_+=1$ and $\mathrm{gr}=0$, the active proof is the direct nine-point
obstruction of Strategy~4.

\subsection{One signed five-row chain}

Use the signed center data of
Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-center-normal-form}.  Put
\[
 X=R-\delta,\qquad
 H=\frac{\eta+\alpha+\delta}{2R},\qquad
 m_3=\min\left\{\frac\alpha R,\frac\delta W\right\},
\]
and
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 c_1&=1-\frac\delta R,&c_2&=1-\delta,&c_3&=1-m_3,\\
 c_4&=1-\alpha,&c_5&=1-\frac\alpha W.
 \end{aligned}
\]
A gap in the normalized right trace starts the same exact chain for CE1 and
CE2:
\begin{equation}
 Z=
 [\widehat g_{c_1}^\vee
 \mid\widehat g_{c_2}^\vee
 \mid\widehat g_{c_3}^\vee
 \mid\widehat g_{c_4}^\vee
 \mid\widehat g_{c_5}^\vee](X).
 \label{eq:reader-exact-five-chain}
\end{equation}
Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-one-gap-interface} connects the formal chain to
actual reaches and gives
\[
 A_0\ge Z,\qquad A_0<1-H.
\]
Thus the exact contradiction is $Z>1-H$.  The first and fifth slots are
extensive, and three capped-map duality steps reduce this target to
\begin{equation}
 [\widehat g_{1-\alpha}^\vee
  \mid\widehat g_{1-m_3}^\vee
  \mid\widehat g_{1-\delta}^\vee](H)
 >1-X=W+\delta.
 \label{eq:reader-signed-three-map}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[CE1 one-gap obstruction]
\label{prop:reader-ce1-one-gap}
The CE1, $N_+=1$, all-$\Vdzero$, one-gap state is impossible.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Here $\Delta_L\le0$.  The exact label analysis first disposes of the Full,
$L$, and $T_-$ ranges.  On the sole surviving selected-$T_+$ ranges, the
universal chord estimates give
\[
 p_1>
 \widehat g_{1-\alpha}^{\vee,\,1-4\alpha}(H)=:L_1,
\]
\[
 p_2>
 \widehat g_{1-m_3}^{\vee,\,1-5m_3}(L_1)=:L_2.
\]
The exact scalar calculation in Appendix~\ref{sec:cyclic-all-vd0} proves
\[
 \delta<\frac1{10},\qquad
 p_2>L_2>2\delta+3\delta^2>e(\delta).
\]
The final threshold map therefore returns a value above $1-X$, proving
\eqref{eq:reader-signed-three-map}.  The exact-row interface then gives
$Z>1-H$, contradicting $A_0<1-H$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[CE2 one-gap obstruction]
\label{prop:reader-ce2-one-gap}
The CE2, $N_+=1$, all-$\Vdzero$, exactly-one-gap state is impossible in either
orientation.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-ce2-one-gap} proves
\[
 X>e(\alpha),\qquad
 \min\{e(\alpha),e(\delta)\}<H.
\]
If $e(\alpha)<H$, use the row-$4$ threshold slot and replace the other four
slots by $\mathrm I$.  Otherwise
$e(\delta)<H\le e(\alpha)<X$, so use the row-$2$ threshold slot.  At least one
slot fires; both may do so.  In either case $Z>1-H$.  Reflection exchanges
$R,W$, exchanges $\alpha,\delta$, and reverses the row order.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The remaining transfer branches}

For $N_+=0$, the all-Vd0 proof is the strict identity cycle, the one-side
endpoint theorem, and Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-common-two-gap}.  The
T3-like branch is Theorem~\ref{thm:paper-complete-t3-endpoint} followed by the
center-free three-row path budget.

For the exactly-one-T3-like $N_+=1$ branch, the translated local profile gives
\[
 a\le1-g_c^{\rm sc},\qquad
 \frac{a}{a+1-u}\le1-g_c^{\rm sc}.
\]
The common center-hiding theorem forces an opposite endpoint reach at least
$g_c^{\rm sc}$, while the unique supercritical row has outgoing reach strictly
below $g_c^{\rm sc}$.  Its four intervening internal edges form the ordinary
center-free path specified in Corollary~\ref{cor:signed-adjacent-rescuers}.

For the one-Vd1/Vd2 branch, the preliminary perimeter budget removes CE1 and
gives
\[
 \alpha+\delta<\frac1{24},\qquad
 \alpha+\delta<\frac{\min\{R,W\}}6.
\]
The adjacent and nonadjacent placements are the exact radial-separation
lemmas in Appendix~\ref{sec:short-vd-placements}.  The remaining Vd1 adjacent
pair is replaced by Proposition~\ref{prop:paper-vd1-pair-replacement}; the
resulting all-Vd0 configuration is impossible.

\begin{proposition}[Branches closed by relaxed \(g\)-chains]
\label{prop:reader-demand-branches}
Strategy~2 closes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the $N_+=0$ all-$\Vdzero$ and T3-like-without-Vd1/Vd2 branches for
      $\CEone$ and $\CEtwo$;
\item every positive-gap $N_+=1$ all-$\Vdzero$ state;
\item the exactly-one-T3-like $N_+=1$ state with no Vd1/Vd2 role;
\item the complementary transfer placements in the $\CEtwo$, $N_+=1$,
      exactly-one-Vd1/Vd2 branch.
\end{enumerate}
Singleton gaps are included.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The first item is Propositions~\ref{prop:nplus-zero-all-vd0} and
\ref{prop:nplus-zero-t3}, with the endpoint proof completed by
Theorem~\ref{thm:paper-complete-t3-endpoint}.  The second is
Propositions~\ref{prop:reader-ce1-one-gap},
\ref{prop:reader-ce2-one-gap}, and
\ref{prop:reader-common-two-gap}.  The third is
Proposition~\ref{prop:nplus-one-one-t3}.  The fourth is
Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-ce2-one-vd-placements}, with its replacement step
supplied by Proposition~\ref{prop:paper-vd1-pair-replacement}.  Every transfer
is connected to actual rows by Lemmas~\ref{lem:reader-raw-capped-transfer} and
\ref{lem:reader-generalized-handoff}.
\end{proof}
