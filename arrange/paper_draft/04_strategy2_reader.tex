\section{Strategy 2: Exact Demand Propagation}
\label{sec:strategy2-reader}

The signed center section supplies the exact CE1/CE2 traces, exits, and
actual-row interface.  Section~\ref{sec:universal-calculus} supplies the
interval residuals, boundary-path budget, selected-$T_+$ curve, and threshold
routing.  The present section contains only the branch-level uses of those
common mechanisms.  Complete contact-cell calculations remain in
Appendix~\ref{sec:exact-local-demand-calculus}.

\subsection{The all-Vd0 gap-rank kernel}

Let $g$ be the number of positive center traces that contain a V-gap.  Then
$g\in\{0,1,2\}$, and $g\le1$ for CE1.  The two all-Vd0 row counts are governed
by the following single matrix:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 &g=0&g=1&g=2\\ \hline
N_+=0&
\text{open-trace budget}&
\text{one-side capped loss}&
\text{paired endpoint loss}\\
N_+=1&
\text{nine-point obstruction}&
\text{five-row transfer}&
\text{paired endpoint loss}.
\end{array}
\]
The paired endpoint column is CE2-only.  The $N_+=0$ row is proved in
Proposition~\ref{prop:nplus-zero-all-vd0}; the $N_+=1$ row is assembled in
Proposition~\ref{prop:nplus-one-all-vd0}.  The common two-gap application is
recorded next.

\begin{proposition}[Common CE2 two-gap application]
\label{prop:reader-common-two-gap}
Assume both CE2 center traces contain V-gaps and rows $T_1,\ldots,T_5$ are
nonsupercritical Vd0 rows.  Then the perimeter is not covered.  No hypothesis
on the criticality of $T_0$ is required.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Put
\[
 p=W-\alpha,
 \qquad
 q=R-\delta.
\]
Gap containment forces the two endpoint inputs $p,q$, while the exact center
exits give radial demands $p/W$ and $q/R$.  Hence the endpoint outputs satisfy
\[
 B_5\le F_{p/W}(p),
 \qquad
 B_1\le F_{q/R}(q).
\]
The exact paired endpoint theorem in
Appendix~\ref{sec:exact-local-demand-calculus} gives
\[
 F_{p/W}(p)+F_{q/R}(q)<1.
\]
Thus $B_1+B_5<1$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:reader-boundary-path}, applied to
$T_2,T_3,T_4$, forces their total boundary contribution above three, contrary
to their three nonsupercritical caps.
\end{proof}

\subsection{One signed five-row chain}

Use the signed center data of
Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-center-normal-form}.  Put
\[
 X=R-\delta,
 \qquad
 H=\frac{\eta+\alpha+\delta}{2R},
 \qquad
 m=\min\left\{\frac\alpha R,\frac\delta W\right\}.
\]
The five nonsupercritical demands are
\[
 1-\frac\delta R,
 \quad1-\delta,
 \quad1-m,
 \quad1-\alpha,
 \quad1-\frac\alpha W.
\]
A gap in the normalized right trace starts the same actual chain
$1\to2\to3\to4\to5\to0$ for CE1 and CE2.  The common interface in
Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-one-gap-interface} reduces both classes to
\begin{equation}
 \left(G_{1-\delta}\circ G_{1-m}\circ G_{1-\alpha}\right)(H)
 >W+\delta.
 \label{eq:reader-signed-three-map}
\end{equation}
The only remaining distinction is the sign of
$\Delta_L=P-R\alpha-\delta$.

\begin{proposition}[CE1 one-gap obstruction]
\label{prop:reader-ce1-one-gap}
The CE1, $N_+=1$, all-$\Vdzero$, one-gap state is impossible.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Here $\Delta_L\le0$.  The low-root estimates give $e(\alpha)<X$ and
$H>\alpha$.  At the row with deficit $\alpha$, the two Full ranges are
impossible.  The $L$ and $T_-$ ranges immediately give an output above
$1-X$.

On the selected $T_+$ range, the center inequalities force $X>1/2$.
Applying the universal chords \eqref{eq:reader-high-chord} and
\eqref{eq:reader-low-chord} first at deficit $\alpha$ and then at deficit $m$
gives
\[
 p_1>(2-4\alpha)H-(1-4\alpha)\alpha=:L_1,
\]
\[
 p_2>(2-5m)L_1-(1-5m)m=:L_2.
\]
The exact terminal calculation in Appendix~\ref{sec:cyclic-all-vd0} proves
\begin{equation}
 \delta<\frac1{10},
 \qquad
 p_2>L_2>2\delta+3\delta^2>e(\delta).
 \label{eq:reader-ce1-terminal}
\end{equation}
Threshold routing at the row with deficit $\delta$ therefore produces an
output above $1-X$, proving \eqref{eq:reader-signed-three-map}.  The common
actual-row interface gives the terminal contradiction.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[CE2 one-gap obstruction]
\label{prop:reader-ce2-one-gap}
The CE2, $N_+=1$, all-$\Vdzero$, exactly-one-gap state is impossible in either
orientation.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
This is Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-ce2-one-gap}.  Its two-threshold proof
uses
\[
 R-\delta>e(\alpha),
 \qquad
 \min\{e(\alpha),e(\delta)\}<H,
\]
and Lemma~\ref{lem:reader-threshold-routing}.  Reflection exchanges
$R$ with $W$, $\alpha$ with $\delta$, and reverses the row order.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The remaining exact-demand branches}

For $N_+=0$, the all-Vd0 proof combines the open-trace rank-zero budget, the
one-side capped-loss theorem, and Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-common-two-gap}.
The T3-like branch first forces and isolates a T3-like row, then applies one
four-label endpoint loss.  Its selected high sheet is precisely the universal
curve \eqref{eq:reader-psi}, with rational parameter
\eqref{eq:reader-beta-rational}.

For the exactly-one-T3-like $N_+=1$ branch, the translated local profile is
rationalized in Appendix~\ref{sec:short-vd-placements}; it verifies the two
hypotheses of the common adjacent-rescuer theorem and no longer repeats the
center-hiding or terminal boundary sum.

For the one-Vd1/Vd2 branch, the preliminary perimeter budget removes CE1 and
gives
\[
 \alpha+\delta<\frac1{24},
 \qquad
 \alpha+\delta<\frac{\min\{R,W\}}6.
\]
The adjacent placement proves the stronger margin $\delta<H/4$ and uses the
global quarter radial envelope.  The nonadjacent placements use the interval
residuals and one Vd corner radial margin.  The remaining Vd1 rescue uses the
same adjacent-rescuer theorem as the T3-like branch.

\begin{proposition}[Branches closed by exact demand]
\label{prop:reader-demand-branches}
Strategy~2 closes all routing-table rows assigned to exact demand propagation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the $N_+=0$ all-$\Vdzero$ and T3-like-without-Vd1/Vd2 branches for
      $\CEone$ and $\CEtwo$;
\item every positive-gap $N_+=1$ all-$\Vdzero$ state;
\item the exactly-one-T3-like $N_+=1$ state with no Vd1/Vd2 role;
\item the complementary exact placements in the $\CEtwo$, $N_+=1$,
      exactly-one-Vd1/Vd2 branch.
\end{enumerate}
Singleton gaps are included.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The first item is Propositions~\ref{prop:nplus-zero-all-vd0} and
\ref{prop:nplus-zero-t3}.  For the second, the one-gap states are
Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-ce1-one-gap} and
Corollary~\ref{prop:reader-ce2-one-gap}; the two-gap state is
Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-common-two-gap}.  The third item is
Proposition~\ref{prop:nplus-one-one-t3}.  The fourth is
Proposition~\ref{prop:signed-ce2-one-vd-placements}.  Every formal transfer is
connected to actual rows by \eqref{eq:reader-actual-propagation} or
\eqref{eq:reader-generalized-transfer}.
\end{proof}
