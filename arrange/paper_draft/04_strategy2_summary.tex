\section{Strategy 2: Propagating Boundary Demand}
\label{sec:strategy2-summary}

Strategy~2 is the only part of the proof in which local information is
propagated through several consecutive vertex roles.  Its geometric content is
simple.  A V triangle that must reach far on its incoming boundary edge and on
its own radial arm has a bounded outgoing reach.  If the next boundary edge is
covered, that outgoing upper bound becomes a lower bound for the next incoming
reach.  Repeating this implication either creates a strict cycle, exhausts the
two endpoint capacities of a center-free path, returns more demand than the
unique supercritical V triangle can supply, or separates two radial traces.

The body deliberately suppresses the formulas producing the numerical
parameters.  Geometry is used here only to select a certificate type and to
produce its parameter record.  Appendix~\ref{sec:strategy2-optimization-problems}
then states each calculation as a real-variable optimization problem, and
Appendix~\ref{sec:strategy2-reader} verifies the required inequalities.  Thus
the reader need not carry the signed center coordinates, contact labels,
radical roots, and relaxation coefficients through the geometric argument.
