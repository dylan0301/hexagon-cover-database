\section{Strategy 3: Area Loss}
\label{sec:strategy3-summary}

Normalize the area of a unit equilateral triangle to one.  Then $H$ has area
six.  For a vertex role with selected adjacent boundary reaches $a,b$, let
$\mathcal L(a,b)$ be the least normalized area forced outside the local
hexagonal wedge.

\begin{proposition}[Area-loss register]
\label{prop:body-area-register}
For every feasible selected pair,
\[
 \mathcal L(a,b)\ge\min\{a,b\}^2.
\]
If $a+b>1$, then
\[
 \mathcal L(a,b)\ge\max\{a,b\}^2.
\]
For a $\Tlike$ role, $a+b\le1$, and for $0\le m\le1/2$,
\[
 a,b\ge m\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \mathcal L(a,b)\ge2m-4m^2.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
These are Theorems~\ref{thm:local-square-loss} and
\ref{thm:t3-direct-loss}.  Appendix~\ref{sec:area} reduces every orientation
to two affine normal forms and proves the inequalities there.  The body uses
only the three displayed losses.
\end{proof}

Suppose strict boundary handoffs are selected and encoded by
\[
 (a_i,b_i)=(1-x_{i-1},x_i),\qquad 0<x_i<1.
\]
Put
\[
 \mu=\min_i x_i,\qquad M=\max_i x_i.
\]
Reflection and reversal replace $x_i$ by $1-x_{-i-1}$, preserve every V triangle
loss, and interchange $\mu$ with $1-M$.  Hence assume $\mu\le1-M$; then
every coordinate $x_i$ and $1-x_i$ is at least $\mu$.

If at least two V triangles are supercritical, one ascent out of a minimum plateau
contributes at least $(1-\mu)^2$, a second ascent contributes strictly more
than $1/4$, and the remaining four V triangles contribute at least $\mu^2$ each.
The total loss is strictly larger than
\[
 4\mu^2+(1-\mu)^2+\frac14
 =5\left(\mu-\frac15\right)^2+\frac{21}{20}>1.
\]
If there is exactly one supercritical V triangle, its unique ascent runs from the
minimum to the maximum.  A distinct $\Tlike$ V triangle then contributes at least
$2\mu-4\mu^2$, the supercritical V triangle contributes at least
$(1-\mu)^2$, and the other four contribute at least $\mu^2$ each.  The sum
is
\[
 (1-\mu)^2+(2\mu-4\mu^2)+4\mu^2=1+\mu^2>1.
\]
Thus the six vertex triangles have total inside area strictly below five; the
center triangle adds at most one.

\begin{proposition}[Branches closed by Strategy 3]
\label{prop:body-area-branches}
Strategy~3 closes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T_C$ is $\CEzero$ and $N_+\ge2$, with arbitrary vertex-role types;
\item $T_C$ is $\CEzero$, $N_+=1$, no role is $\Vdone$ or $\Vdtwo$, and at
least one role is $\Tlike$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In CE0 the six vertex roles cover the boundary, so
Proposition~\ref{prop:body-structural-reduction} supplies strict selected
handoffs preserving the actual supercritical pattern.  The two cyclic sums
above apply.  This is Proposition~\ref{prop:area-branches}, whose local
orientation calculations remain in Appendix~\ref{sec:area}.
\end{proof}
