\subsection{The explicit zero-gap nine-point obstruction}
\label{subsec:nine-point}

The explicit zero-gap member of the finite-enclosure strategy applies when all six V triangles are Vd0, the
boundary is covered, and exactly one V triangle is supercritical.  Six
radial points and three asymmetric frontier points are forced into the center
triangle.  The detailed construction is followed by the support-arc covering
argument and the final enclosure contradiction.
