\subsection{Zero-Gap Nine-Point Obstruction}
\label{sec:strategy4-reader}

This section keeps the reader-facing obstruction argument visible.  The
appendix contains the detailed optimization and certificate computations, but
the main text retains the geometric meaning of the witness construction: a
hypothetical zero-gap configuration forces a compact set inside the central
triangle that cannot be enclosed by any open unit equilateral triangle.

The role of the following definitions is therefore not only to provide symbols,
but also to expose the geometric route of the contradiction: first force a
central disk and asymmetric boundary witnesses into $U_C$, then prove that the
resulting compact witness has enclosure number at least one.
