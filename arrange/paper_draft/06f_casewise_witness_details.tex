\subsection{Casewise construction of the forced finite sets}
\label{subsec:new-casewise-witness-details}

This subsection records the exact forced object used by each nonzero-gap row.

\subsubsection{One gap and no supercritical V role}

Normalize the gap to
\[
 J=[B_0,1-A_1]=[\ell,r]\subset e_{0,1},
\]
and put $p=1-r$, $q=\ell$.  Handoffs on the other five edges and
nonsupercriticality show that every local pair dominates $(p,q)$.
Let $c_*=c_{\max}(p,q)$.  Type-aware radial forcing gives
\[
 (1-c_*)V_i\in U_C
 \qquad(0\le i\le5).
 \tag{6.72}
\]
Their convex hull contains $\mathcal D_{h(1-c_*)}$, while the actual gap is
exactly $J(p,q)$.  This is the complementary-gap terminal.

The same construction works with one or two T3-like roles because the
permitted neighboring capacity is bounded by $c_*$.

\subsubsection{Two gaps}

For CE2, put
\[
 p=W-\alpha,\qquad q=R-\delta.
\]
The four center-free handoffs between the two gap edges show that
$T_1,\ldots,T_5$ dominate $(p,q)$.  Therefore
\[
 D_2=(1-c_{\max}(p,q))V_2,
 \qquad
 D_4=(1-c_{\max}(p,q))V_4
 \tag{6.73}
\]
are missed by all V roles.  The shortened CE2 theorem proves that one lies
beyond the corresponding C exit.  This argument is independent of the
criticality of $T_0$ and permits the type-aware T3-like neighboring terms.

\subsubsection{One gap, one supercritical Vd0 role}

Let $C_i$ be the actual own-radial reach and
$P_i=(1-C_i)V_i$.  Every $P_i$ is center-forced, but the enclosure
certificate retains only
\[
 \boxed{
 K_{\rm tr}
 =
 \{O,M_0,X(\ell),X(r),P_2,P_3,P_4\}.
 }
 \tag{6.74}
\]
Suppose an open unit triangle $U'$ contains this set.  Its actual gap trace
gives, with
\[
 Q=\frac{\eta+\alpha+\delta}{2R},
\]
the endpoint-diameter upper squeeze
\[
 A_0<1-Q.
\]
Containment of $P_4,P_3,P_2$ supplies the three radial demands used by the
CE1 transverse return.  In CE2, $P_4,P_2$ supply the two threshold demands.
Both center classes force
\[
 A_0>1-Q.
\]
Thus $\Lambda(K_{\rm tr})\ge1$.  The former points $P_0,P_1,P_5$ are not
needed.

\subsubsection{The T3-like rescuer endpoint}

Write the T3-like supported interval on $r_1$ as $[c,u]$.  The point
\[
 P_T=(1-u)V_1
\]
is missed by the open T3-like role, the adjacent supercritical role, and all
Vd0 or nonlocal roles.  Thus $P_T\in U_C$.

The T3 normal form proves
\[
 a\le1-M_c^{\rm sup},
 \qquad
 \frac{a}{a+1-u}\le1-M_c^{\rm sup},
 \qquad
 a+1-u\le1.
\]
These are precisely the hypotheses of the common rescuer-tail budget.  No
T3-specific center-hiding calculation remains.

\subsubsection{The Vd radial points}

In the adjacent placement, the Vd supported endpoint lies below
$1-\rho_L$.  The ordinary role $T_2$ has boundary demands
\[
 \left(\frac12+\rho_R,\rho_L\right).
\]
The one-third radial envelope gives
\[
 C_2<1-\frac{\rho_L}{3}<1-\delta.
\]
Therefore a point of $r_2$ between the two local V traces and the C interval
is uncovered.

In the nonadjacent placement,
\[
 D_\tau=\min\{\rho_R,\rho_L\}V_\tau
\]
lies beyond the Vd own trace and beyond every relevant C exit.

In the Vd1 neighboring-midpoint placement, the O-side supported endpoint is
center-forced and the two local inequalities are routed to the same
rescuer-tail budget used by the T3-like case.

\subsubsection{The corrected replacement placement}

When neither distinguished role is based at $V_0$, the corrected Vd1
two-chart replacement preserves the covered skeleton and produces six
nonsupercritical Vd0 roles.  Recompute the output gap rank.  Rank zero uses
the boundary-complete length theorem, rank one the common radial disk and
actual gap, and rank two the shortened CE2 theorem.  No preservation of the
input gap rank is needed.
