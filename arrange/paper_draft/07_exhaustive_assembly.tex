\section{Completion of the Proof}
\label{sec:assembly}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Assume that seven open unit equilateral triangles cover $H$.  By
Proposition~\ref{prop:exhaustive-structural-reduction}, the cover lies in
exactly one row of Table~\ref{tab:routing}.  The rows assigned to trace length
are excluded by Proposition~\ref{prop:length-branches}; the rows assigned to
area loss by Proposition~\ref{prop:area-branches}; the boundary-reach
propagation rows by Proposition~\ref{prop:demand-branches}, including the
complete CE2 one-$\Vdone/\Vdtwo$ positional assembly in
Proposition~\ref{prop:paper-ce2-one-vd-placements}; and the no-gap,
all-$\Vdzero$ rows by Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-ab-core-branches}.  Hence
every row is impossible, contradicting the assumed cover.
\end{proof}

Corollary~\ref{cor:expanded-closed} follows from
Proposition~\ref{prop:open-closed-scaled}.
