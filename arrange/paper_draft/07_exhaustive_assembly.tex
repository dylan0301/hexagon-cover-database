\section{Exhaustive Assembly}
\label{sec:assembly}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Assume that seven open unit equilateral triangles cover $H$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:body-structural-reduction} assigns the cover to exactly
one row of Table~\ref{tab:routing}, with $N_+$ computed from the actual reaches
and, in the CE1/CE2 branch, $q=N_++m$.

The trace rows contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:body-length-branches}.  The
area rows contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:body-area-branches}.  The transfer
rows contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:body-demand-branches}; their retained
maps and terminal inequalities are visible in
Table~\ref{tab:body-strategy2-chains}.  The convex-support rows contradict
Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-ab-core-branches}.

In the sole hybrid row marked $1+2$, Strategy~1 closes the neighboring-midpoint
placement and Strategy~2 closes the complementary placements.  No routing row
is omitted and no actual criticality classification is inferred from selected
demands.  Hence every row of Table~\ref{tab:routing} is impossible, a
contradiction.
\end{proof}

Corollary~\ref{cor:expanded-closed} follows from
Proposition~\ref{prop:open-closed-scaled}.
