\section{Completion of the Proof}
\label{sec:assembly}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Suppose seven open unit equilateral triangles cover $H$.
By \zcref{thm:fixed-nzero}, $N_+\ge1$.
With no gaps, the nine-point obstruction \zcref{thm:reader-zero-gap-obstruction}
excludes $N_+=1$, and \zcref{prop:area-branches} excludes $N_+\ge2$.

With a gap, \zcref{lem:midpoint-supplier} gives
$N_+=1,N_{\rm sp}\le1$. Let $M_k$ be the C midpoint and $\sigma$
the supercritical index. The case $\sigma=k$ is excluded by
\zcref{thm:center-aligned-path}. Otherwise $M_\sigma$ has a unique
adjacent positive-support supplier. A T3-like supplier is center-based
and is excluded by \zcref{prop:new-one-t3-terminal}; a Vd2 supplier
is excluded by \zcref{prop:ce2-vd2-midpoint-length}. For Vd1, a
center-based supplier uses D, whereas an away supplier admits replacement
followed by N0, as proved in \zcref{prop:new-one-vd-assembly}.
Thus no cover exists. The scaling equivalence
\zcref{prop:open-closed-scaled} gives \zcref{cor:expanded-closed}.
\end{proof}
