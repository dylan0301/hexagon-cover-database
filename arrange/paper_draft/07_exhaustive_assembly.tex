\section{Completion of the Proof}
\label{sec:assembly}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Suppose seven open unit equilateral triangles cover $H$. Their center and
vertex roles are distinct. By \zcref{thm:fixed-nzero}, $N_+\ge1$.

If there are no actual gaps, the V triangles cover the perimeter.
Exactly one supercritical role is excluded by the type-independent
nine-point theorem \zcref{thm:reader-zero-gap-obstruction}; at least two
are excluded by \zcref{prop:area-branches}.

If there is a gap, \zcref{lem:midpoint-supplier} reduces the cover to
$N_+=1,N_{\rm sp}\le1$. Let $M_k$ be the C midpoint and $\sigma$ the
supercritical index. If $\sigma=k$, apply \zcref{thm:center-aligned-path}.
Otherwise $M_\sigma$ has a unique adjacent positive-support supplier.
A T3-like supplier must be based at $V_k$ and is excluded by
\zcref{prop:new-one-t3-terminal}. A Vd2 supplier is excluded by
\zcref{prop:ce2-vd2-midpoint-length}. A center-based Vd1 supplier uses D;
an away Vd1 supplier admits the two-vertex replacement and contradicts N0.
These last alternatives are assembled in \zcref{prop:new-one-vd-assembly}.
Thus every possibility contradicts the cover.
\end{proof}

The zero-gap certificate uses coverage of all of $H$. Replacement needs
only skeleton coverage and is never used to invoke that certificate.
Corollary~\ref{cor:expanded-closed} follows from
Proposition~\ref{prop:open-closed-scaled}.
