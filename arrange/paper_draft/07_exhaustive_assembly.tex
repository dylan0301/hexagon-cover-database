\section{Exhaustive Assembly}
\label{sec:assembly}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Assume that seven open unit equilateral triangles cover $H$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:body-structural-reduction} assigns the cover to exactly
one row of Table~\ref{tab:routing}, with $N_+$ computed from actual maximal
reaches and, in the CE1/CE2 branch, $q=N_++m$.

The Strategy~1 rows contradict
Proposition~\ref{prop:body-length-branches}.  The Strategy~3 rows contradict
Proposition~\ref{prop:body-area-branches}.  The all-Vd0, T3-like, and ordinary
transfer rows contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:body-demand-branches}; their
exact retained maps and terminal inequalities are recorded in
Table~\ref{tab:body-strategy2-chains}.  The complete CE2 exactly-one-Vd1/Vd2
row, including the neighboring-midpoint length subcase and every complementary
placement, contradicts Proposition~\ref{prop:paper-ce2-one-vd-placements}.
The zero-gap all-Vd0 rows contradict
Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-ab-core-branches}; its two mixed cap overlaps are
Theorem~\ref{thm:paper-exact-mixed-certificate}.

No routing row is omitted.  Singleton gaps remain gaps, every center-assisted
handoff is treated by a residual map, every identity path has center-free
internal edges, and no actual criticality classification is inferred from a
selected demand pair.  Hence every row of Table~\ref{tab:routing} is
impossible, a contradiction.
\end{proof}

Corollary~\ref{cor:expanded-closed} follows from
Proposition~\ref{prop:open-closed-scaled}.
