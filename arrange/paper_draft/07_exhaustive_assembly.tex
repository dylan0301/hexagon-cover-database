\section{Exhaustive Assembly}
\label{sec:assembly}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Assume that seven open unit equilateral triangles cover $H$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:exhaustive-structural-reduction} assigns the cover to
exactly one row of Table~\ref{tab:routing}, with $N_+$ computed from the
actual reaches.  The rows assigned to Strategies~1, 2, 3, and 4 contradict,
respectively, Propositions~\ref{prop:length-branches},
\ref{prop:reader-demand-branches}, \ref{prop:area-branches}, and
\ref{prop:reader-ab-core-branches}.  In the sole row marked $1+2$, the first
two propositions cover the complementary subcases stated in their
conclusions.  Thus every row is impossible.
\end{proof}

Corollary~\ref{cor:expanded-closed} follows from
Proposition~\ref{prop:open-closed-scaled}.
