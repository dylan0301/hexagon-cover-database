\section{Exhaustive Assembly}
\label{sec:assembly}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Assume that seven open unit equilateral triangles cover $H$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:exhaustive-structural-reduction} assigns the cover to
exactly one row of Table~\ref{tab:routing}, with $N_+$ computed from the
actual reaches and, in the CE1/CE2 branch, $q=N_++m$.

The additive trace rows contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:length-branches},
and the additive area rows contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:area-branches}.
The isotone transfer rows contradict
Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-demand-branches}, whose all-Vd0 subcases are the
$2\times3$ gap-rank kernel of Section~\ref{sec:strategy2-reader}.  The convex
support rows contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-ab-core-branches}.
In the sole hybrid row marked $1+2$, the trace and transfer propositions cover
the complementary placements stated in their conclusions.  Thus every row is
impossible.
\end{proof}

Corollary~\ref{cor:expanded-closed} follows from
Proposition~\ref{prop:open-closed-scaled}.
