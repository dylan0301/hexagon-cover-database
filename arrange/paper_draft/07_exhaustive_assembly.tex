\section{Exhaustive Assembly}
\label{sec:assembly}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
Assume that seven open unit equilateral triangles cover $H$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:exhaustive-structural-reduction} assigns the cover to
exactly one row of Table~\ref{tab:routing}, with $N_+$ computed from the
actual reaches and, in the CE1/CE2 branch, $q=N_++m$.

The additive trace rows contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:length-branches},
and the additive area rows contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:area-branches}.
The isotone transfer rows contradict
Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-demand-branches}. Their exact seeds, hatted or
center-assisted row maps, relaxed slots, and terminal certificates are audited
in Table~\ref{tab:strategy2-chain-signatures}; the two all-Vd0 row counts are
the $2\times3$ active-gap-rank kernel of
Section~\ref{sec:strategy2-reader}. The convex support rows contradict
Proposition~\ref{prop:reader-ab-core-branches}.

In the sole hybrid row marked $1+2$, the existing Strategy~1 trace proposition
and the Strategy~2 transfer proposition cover the complementary placements
stated in their conclusions. No Strategy~1 routing assignment is absorbed or
removed by the $g$-chain reformulation. Thus every row is impossible.
\end{proof}

Corollary~\ref{cor:expanded-closed} follows from
Proposition~\ref{prop:open-closed-scaled}.
