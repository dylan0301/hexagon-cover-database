\section{Guide to the Technical Proofs}
\label{sec:appendix-roadmap}

The reader proof above states every geometric quantity and every inequality
used in the final routing.  The appendices prove those statements in the
following order.

Appendix~\ref{sec:structural-reductions} proves the open--closed scaling
equivalence, the center and vertex classifications, strict handoff selection,
and the exhaustive case split.  Appendices~\ref{sec:universal-calculus} and
\ref{sec:admissible-set-derivation} establish the equilateral enclosure gauge,
the exact local admissible set, and the propagation inequalities.
Appendix~\ref{sec:signed-ce12-reductions} derives the signed-center parameters,
trace intervals, and radial exits.  Appendices~\ref{sec:length} and the common
CE1/CE2 length calculations prove the trace bounds.

Appendix~\ref{sec:strategy2-parameter-bridges} derives the finite real
parameters used in the boundary-reach arguments.
Appendix~\ref{sec:strategy2-optimization-problems} states the explicit
real-variable inequalities, and Section~\ref{sec:strategy2-pure-theorems}
proves them and applies them to the geometric cases.  Appendix~\ref{sec:area}
proves the local and cyclic area-loss estimates.  Appendix~\ref{sec:ab-core}
proves the nine-point geometry, rational inner approximants, and equal-radius
support-arc intersections.  Finally,
Appendix~\ref{sec:strategy4-exact-certificate} proves the two unequal-radius
intersections by exact symbolic arithmetic and the authenticated
Bernstein-positivity certificate distributed with the paper.
