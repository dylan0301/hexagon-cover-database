\section{Technical Verification Roadmap}
\label{sec:appendix-roadmap}

The body has already completed the logical proof.  The remaining sections
verify its interfaces.  Except for the finite structural classification, every
calculation is organized as an inequality problem on explicit real-variable
domains.

Geometric notation is retained only long enough to define the relevant real
variables.  Polynomial expansions, root selection, rational parametrizations,
monotonicity checks, Gram reductions, and Bernstein identities occur only
below.  Exact scripts are replay tools; the mathematical certificate is the
stated rational identity or sign decomposition, not the invocation of a
program.
