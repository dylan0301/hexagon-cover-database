\section{Nonstandard Symbols}
\label{app:symbols}

Standard geometric notation is defined at first use.  The following symbols
recur across proof mechanisms.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.27\textwidth}p{.66\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
symbol&meaning\\
\midrule
$\CEzero,\CEone,\CEtwo$&
The three exhaustive center-role boundary-trace classes.\\
$\Vdzero,\Vdone,\Vdtwo,\Tlike$&
The four exhaustive vertex-role classes.\\
$(A_i,B_i,C_i)$&
Actual maximal incoming boundary, outgoing boundary, and radial reaches.\\
$(a_i,b_i,c_i)$&
Selected lower-bound demands; these do not define $N_+$.\\
$N_+,m,q$&
The number of actual supercritical rows, the number of non-Vd0 roles, and the
number of short roles; in CE1/CE2 one has $q=N_++m$.\\
$g$&
The number of positive center traces containing active V-gaps; $g\in\{0,1,2\}$
and $g\le1$ in CE1.\\
$\Lambda(K)$&
The least side length of a closed equilateral triangle containing the compact
set $K$.\\
$\omega(x),\sigma(x)$&
The universal equilateral radical $\sqrt{1-x+x^2}$ and its deficit
$1-\sqrt{1-x+x^2}$.\\
$\mathcal R_J(p)$&
The far-side residual boundary demand after an initial trace $[0,p]$ and a
center interval $J$.\\
$B_c,F_c,G_c$&
The outgoing feasible envelope, its nonsupercritical cap, and the no-center
transfer map $G_c=1-F_c$.\\
$\mathcal G_{c,J}$&
The generalized center-assisted transfer
$\mathcal R_J\mathbin{\circ}F_c$; it equals $G_c$ when $J=\varnothing$.\\
$e(d)$&
The high-radial low-root threshold for deficit $d$.\\
$R,W,E,\alpha,\delta,\Delta_R,\Delta_L$&
The common signed CE1/CE2 center variables and trace surpluses.\\
$f(a,b),\mathcal L(a,b)$&
Normalized maximal retained area and the corresponding local area loss
$1-f(a,b)$.\\
$c_*,\mathcal D_\eta,Q_-,Q_0,Q_+$&
The radial envelope, forced disk, and exact asymmetric witnesses in
Strategy~4.\\
$A,B,C,\widehat K,K_{\rm wit}$&
The Newton inner points, inner witness hull, and exact forced witness set.\\
$I(X,r)$&
The level-$\sqrt3/2$ support cap associated with the disk
$\mathbb D(X,r)$.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
