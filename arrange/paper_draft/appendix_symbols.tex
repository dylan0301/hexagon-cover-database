\section{Nonstandard Symbols}
\label{app:symbols}

Standard geometric notation is defined at first use.  The following symbols
recur across proof mechanisms.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.28\textwidth}p{.65\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
symbol&meaning\\
\midrule
$\CEzero,\CEone,\CEtwo$&
The three exhaustive center-role boundary-trace classes.\\
$\Vdzero,\Vdone,\Vdtwo,\Tlike$&
The four exhaustive vertex-role classes.\\
$A(T),B(T),C(T)$&
Functions returning the actual maximal incoming-boundary, outgoing-boundary,
and own-radial reaches of a tagged V triangle;
$(A_i,B_i,C_i)$ are the compact indexed aliases.\\
$(a_i,b_i,c_i)$&
Selected lower-bound demands; these do not define $N_+$.\\
$d_i^C,c_i^C$&
The center exit measured from $O$ and its complementary vertex-side radial
demand $c_i^C=1-d_i^C$.\\
$m_3$&
The local CE1 affine-slot scalar $m_3=d_3^C$; it is distinct from the
paper-wide role count $m$.\\
$N_+,m,q$&
The number of actual supercritical V triangles, non-Vd0 roles, and short roles; in
CE1/CE2 one has $q=N_++m$.\\
$\mathrm{gr}$&
The active-gap rank; bare $g$ is reserved for transfer maps.\\
$\Lambda(K)$&
The least side length of a closed equilateral triangle containing $K$.\\
$\omega(x),\sigma(x)$&
The radical $\sqrt{1-x+x^2}$ and its deficit $1-\sqrt{1-x+x^2}$.\\
$\mathcal A$&
The exact local admissible set of boundary--radial demand triples.\\
$g_c(x)$&
The historical raw defect map:
$\max\{y:(1-x,y,c)\in\mathcal A\}$.\\
$\widehat g_c(x)$&
The nonsupercritical cap $\min\{g_c(x),x\}$.\\
$f^\vee(a)$&
The complement dual $1-f(1-a)$; in particular
$\widehat g_c^\vee$ is the extensive incoming-reach transfer.\\
$\mathcal R_J(p)$&
The far-side residual demand after an initial trace and center interval $J$.\\
$g_{c,J}^\vee,\widehat g_{c,J}^\vee$&
The raw and capped center-assisted reach transfers.\\
$g_c^{\rm sc}$&
The single free strict-supercritical outgoing envelope
$\sup_{\{x:g_c(x)>x\}}g_c(x)$.\\
$\widehat g_{1-d}^{\vee,\lambda}$&
A certified affine selected-$T_+$ lower relaxation.\\
$\widehat g_{1-d}^{\vee,\rm th}$&
The low-root threshold lower relaxation.\\
$[\Phi_1\mid\cdots\mid\Phi_r]$&
The composition $\Phi_r\circ\cdots\circ\Phi_1$, with maps listed in geometric
V-triangle order.\\
$B_c,F_c,G_c$&
Technical verification aliases:
$B_c(a)=g_c(1-a)$,
$F_c(a)=\widehat g_c(1-a)$, and
$G_c(a)=\widehat g_c^\vee(a)$.\\
$F(c,x),T(c,x)$&
The pure Strategy~2 optimization functions:
$F(c,x)$ is the explicit capped outgoing capacity and
$T(c,x)=1-F(c,x)$ is the next-input update.  They occur only in
Appendix~\ref{sec:strategy2-optimization-problems}.\\
$\mathcal D_1,\mathcal D_2$&
The pure CE1 and CE2 signed parameter domains in variables $(r,a,d)$.\\
$\Psi_{\rm E1},\Psi_{\rm E2},\Psi_{\rm R1},\Psi_{\rm R2}$&
The four formalization-ready Strategy~2 objective functions.\\
$e(d)$&
The high-radial low-root threshold for deficit $d$ in the geometric and
verification notation.\\
$R,W,E,\alpha,\delta,\Delta_R,\Delta_L$&
The common signed CE1/CE2 center variables and trace surpluses.\\
$f(a,b),\mathcal L(a,b)$&
Normalized maximal retained area and the corresponding local area loss.\\
$c_*,\mathcal D_\eta,Q_-,Q_0,Q_+$&
The radial envelope, forced disk, and exact asymmetric witnesses in
Strategy~4.\\
$A,B,C,\widehat K,K_{\rm wit}$&
The Newton inner points, inner witness hull, and exact forced witness set.\\
$I(X,r)$&
The level-$\sqrt3/2$ support cap associated with the disk
$\mathbb D(X,r)$.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
