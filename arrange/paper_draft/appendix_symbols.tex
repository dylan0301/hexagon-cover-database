\section{Notation Index}
\label{app:symbols}

The global geometric notation is summarized in Section~\ref{sec:global-notation}.
The following additional symbols occur in the technical proofs.

$A(T),B(T),C(T)$&
The maximal backward-boundary, forward-boundary, and radial reaches of a
vertex triangle; $(A_i,B_i,C_i)$ are the indexed aliases.\\
$(a_i,b_i,c_i)$&
Selected reach lower bounds; these never define $N_+$.\\
$d_i^C,c_i^C$&
The center exit measured from $O$ and the complementary radial lower bound
$c_i^C=1-d_i^C$.\\
$N_{\mathrm{sp}}$&
The number of vertex triangles of type $\Vdone,\Vdtwo,$ or $\Tlike$.\\
$N_{\mathrm{gap}}$&
The number of positive center traces containing a boundary gap.\\
$\Lambda(K)$&
The least side length of a closed equilateral triangle containing $K$.\\
$\omega(x),\sigma(x)$&
The radical $\sqrt{1-x+x^2}$ and its deficit
$1-\sqrt{1-x+x^2}$.\\
$\mathcal A$&
The exact local admissible set of backward, forward, and radial reach triples.\\
$M_c(a)$&
The maximal possible forward reach when the backward and radial reaches are at
least $a$ and $c$.\\
$\Mbar_c(a)$&
The nonsupercritical cap $\min\{M_c(a),1-a\}$.\\
$\Phi_c(a)$&
The center-free propagated lower bound $1-\Mbar_c(a)$.\\
$\rho_J(b)$&
The uncovered suffix after an initial trace of length at most $b$ and a center
interval $J$.\\
$\Phi_{c,J}(a)$&
The center-assisted propagated lower bound
$\rho_J(\Mbar_c(a))$.\\
$M_c^{\rm sup}$&
The strict-supercritical forward-reach envelope.\\
$g_c(x)$&
Technical complementary-coordinate alias $g_c(x)=M_c(1-x)$.\\
$f^\vee(a)$&
The technical complement dual $1-f(1-a)$.\\
$\mathcal R_J(p)$&
Technical appendix alias for the uncovered suffix; in reader notation it is
$\rho_J(p)$.\\
$B_c,F_c,G_c$&
Technical verification aliases:
$B_c(a)=M_c(a)$,
$F_c(a)=\Mbar_c(a)$, and
$G_c(a)=1-F_c(a)$.\\
$F(c,x),T(c,x)$&
The high-radial output function and complementary update used in the finite
real-variable inequalities.\\
$\mathcal D_{\rm sgn}$&
The signed-center parameter domain.\\
$\mathcal D_{\rm C1},\mathcal D_{\rm C2}$&
The full CE1 and CE2 signed-center cells.\\
$\mathcal D_1,\mathcal D_2$&
The oriented CE1 one-gap cell and the CE2 cell used in the all-$\Vdzero$
inequalities.\\
$R,W,E,\alpha,\delta,\Delta_R,\Delta_L$&
The common signed CE1/CE2 center variables and trace surpluses.\\
$\Psi_{\rm E1},\Psi_{\rm E2},\Psi_{\rm R1},\Psi_{\rm R2}$&
The four all-$\Vdzero$ real-variable objective functions.\\
$\Omega_{\rm T3},\Omega_{\rm SC},\Omega_{\rm VD}$&
The finite real-variable domains for the $\Tlike$, adjacent exceptional, and
Vd inequalities.\\
$\mathsf E(L,U;x),\mathsf R(L,U;x)$&
Technical three-cell component endpoint and complementary suffix functions.\\
$e(d)$&
The high-radial low-root threshold for deficit $d$.\\
$f(a,b),\mathcal L(a,b)$&
Normalized maximal retained area and the corresponding local area loss.\\
$c_\ast,r_\ast,P_i^{\rm rad},Q_-,Q_0,Q_+$&
The radial envelope, forced disk radius, six radial points, and three exact
frontier points in the nine-point argument.\\
$P_-,P_0,P_+,\widehat K,K_{\rm wit}$&
The rational inner approximants, inner witness hull, and exact forced witness
set.\\
$\operatorname{Cap}(X,r)$&
The level-$\sqrt3/2$ support arc associated with the disk
$\mathbb B(X,r)$.\\
\end{longtable}
