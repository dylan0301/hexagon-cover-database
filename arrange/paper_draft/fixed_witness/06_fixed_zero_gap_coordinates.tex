\paragraph{Explicit construction of the zero-gap points and disks.}
One may choose every strict handoff explicitly as
\[
 \xi_i=\frac{1-A_{i+1}+B_i}{2}.
\]
The exact-one handoff lemma preserves the supercritical index under this
choice. At index $4$ use the outgoing/incoming chart
\[
 \Psi_4(u,v)=V_4+u(V_5-V_4)+v(V_3-V_4).
\]
Put $\rho=a^2+ab+b^2$, $\Delta=\sqrt{4\rho-3}$ and define
\begin{align*}
 \alpha_F&=\frac{h(b+2a-b\Delta)}{2\rho},&
 \beta_F&=\frac{h(b-a+(a+b)\Delta)}{2\rho},\\
 \gamma_F&=\frac{h(-b+a+(a+b)\Delta)}{2\rho},&
 \delta_F&=\frac{h(2b+a-a\Delta)}{2\rho}.
\end{align*}
These are frontier coefficients, not the signed C-triangle parameters.
The selected first-root parameters are
\begin{align*}
 \lambda_-&=2(\alpha_F+b\beta_F)
 -\frac23\sqrt{9(\alpha_F+b\beta_F)^2-9b^2+9b-6},\\
 \mu_+&=2(\delta_F+a\gamma_F)
 -\frac23\sqrt{9(\delta_F+a\gamma_F)^2-9a^2+9a-6}.
\end{align*}
Then the same three frontier witnesses are
\[
 Q_-=\Psi_4(b-\beta_F\lambda_-,\alpha_F\lambda_-),
 \qquad Q_+=\Psi_4(\delta_F\mu_+,a-\gamma_F\mu_+),
\]
\[
 Q_0=\Psi_4\left(
 \frac{2b+a-a\Delta}{\Delta+3},
 \frac{b+2a-b\Delta}{\Delta+3}\right).
\]
The exact fixed-line calculation ensures that the radicals are real and
selects the stated roots. No sampled point or new root selection is used.
The finite set and its compact comparison set are
\[
 K_F=\{(1-c_*)V_i:0\le i\le5\}\cup\{Q_-,Q_0,Q_+\},
\]
\[
 \mathcal D_F=\{x:\|x\|\le h(1-c_{\max}(1-b,1-a))\},
 \qquad \mathcal D_F\cup\{Q_-,Q_0,Q_+\}\subseteq\conv(K_F).
\]
When $c_*\le2/3$, the six radial points already suffice because
$\Lambda(\mathcal D_F)=3(1-c_*)\ge1$. For $c_*>2/3$, the unchanged
asymmetric enclosure certificate is needed.

Its smaller comparison disk is also explicit. With $p=1-b$, $q=1-a$,
$s=p+q$, $m=\min\{p,q\}$, and
$\chi=s^4-s^2+pq$, put
\[
 \bar c=\begin{cases}
 s-\dfrac{\chi}{4s^3-2s+m},&\chi\le0,\\[2mm]
 s-\dfrac{\chi}{1-m},&\chi>0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Then
\[
 \overline{\mathcal D}_F=\{x:\|x\|\le h(1-\bar c)\}
 \subseteq\mathcal D_F,
\]
using the exact bound $c_*\le\bar c<1$. This smaller disk is a
comparison inside the tangent-residual certificate, not an additional
global witness recipe. The two unit disks defining the frontier circles
are $\mathbb B(X_2(b),1)$ and $\mathbb B(X_5(1-a),1)$; they are auxiliary
distance constructions, not disks asserted to lie in the C triangle.
