There is exactly one normalized unit of excess area: seven triangles are
available for a hexagon of area six. Thus a total loss greater than one
outside $H$ makes a cover impossible, even before accounting for overlap
inside $H$. The local estimate quantifies the loss forced by a V triangle's
two boundary anchors. The cyclic step is used only when the V triangles
cover the whole boundary; it links their anchors through the strict
handoffs. The area argument does not assume a particular C triangle type.
