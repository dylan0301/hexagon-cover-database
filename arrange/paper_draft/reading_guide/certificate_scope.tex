This is the algebraic verification stage of the enclosure proof. The input
is the parameter domain and the residual inequalities derived from the
supporting-contact geometry, not a list of sampled covers. The finite data
encode exact identities and positivity certificates; the replay programs
check those identities using exact arithmetic. The geometric reduction and
the validity of its domain remain part of the written proof. A successful
replay certifies the specified polynomial step, not an independent formal
verification of the entire theorem.
