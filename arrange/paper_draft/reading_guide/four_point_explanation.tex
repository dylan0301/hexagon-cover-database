\readerheading{Why four D points suffice.}
The two gap endpoints and the forced supported-ray endpoint are fixed from
the original triangles. The geometric condition is only
$\beta\le\varepsilon/(a+\varepsilon)$. If $s=a+\varepsilon\ge1$,
a pair has squared distance $1-s+s^2\ge1$. For $0<s<1$, put $v=a/s$:
convexity allows the far endpoint to move inward to $Y(v)$, and the four
resulting hull-edge calipers all have side greater than one.
The original supplier charts still establish forcing and the ratio; no
candidate center normalization is used in the enclosure calculation.
