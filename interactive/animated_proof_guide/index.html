How to read these examples. A terminal proof branch assumes a cover and derives a contradiction; no example can realize all those hypotheses simultaneously. Local triangles, independent area-optimizer candidates, and witness constructions are therefore labeled separately. Uppercase A, B, C are actual maximal reaches; lowercase a, b, c are selected demands. N+ always counts actual A+B>1. Open U triangles and closed T triangles must not be confused.

The complete terminal route

First prove N+ ≥ 1. For a hypothetical cover, use actual boundary gaps—including singletons.

No gaps

N+ = 1 → F: nine points.

N+ ≥ 2 → cyclic area loss.

F splits into disk-only range and Newton / four-contact range.

At least one gap

Length budgets reduce to N+=1, Nsp≤1. Let M_k be the C midpoint and sigma the unique supercritical index.

sigma = k → BC (one or two gaps).

sigma ≠ k → a unique adjacent midpoint supplier: T3-like → D; Vd2 → perimeter; Vd1 at k → D; Vd1 away from k → two-chart replacement → N0.
