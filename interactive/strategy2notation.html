Step 01 · skeleton and roles

Seven distinguished points give seven roles

The center role TC owns O; the six vertex roles Ti own Vi. Strategy 2 propagates boundary information around this fixed cyclic order.

S = ∂H ∪ ⋃i=05 ri

Highlight the skeleton

Toggle layers

The routing coordinates

indices modulo 6
Ti: Ai incoming · Bi outgoing · Ci radial

Ai, Bi, Ci are the actual maximal reaches of one vertex role. On a center-free covered edge, the handoff is

Bi + Ai+1 ≥ 1.

The role is supercritical exactly when Ai+Bi>1. The count of such actual roles is N+.

Role classes—CE1/CE2 at the center and Vd0/Vd1/Vd2/T3-like at the vertices—describe actual triangles, never a smaller selected demand triple.
