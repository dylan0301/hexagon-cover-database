# Reusable lemma catalog

Status: Reference

The active proof has three geometric mechanisms: length, area, and finite
point enclosure. Navigation is not a mathematical authority. All claims
below are owned by numbered Proven sources.

## Minimal route

The main proof invokes N0 first. For zero actual gaps, one supercritical
role uses the type-independent nine-point theorem and at least two use
cyclic area loss. With a gap, the skeleton count and midpoint-supplier
lemma leave one supercritical role and at most one positive-support role.
The relative position of this role and its supplier, not the complete
V-type pattern, selects B/C, D, replacement, or the Vd2 perimeter exit.

## Four active witness sets

B has at most four points, C at most six, D at most four, and F at most
nine. Only F uses a disk in the minimal route. A, with at most seven
points and a disk, remains an independent complementary-gap alternative.
The actual total endpoints include every permitted neighboring trace;
an own endpoint is not substituted for a total endpoint. Witness sets are
fixed before an arbitrary candidate triangle is introduced. Replacement
preserves the skeleton but need not preserve the input gap rank or cover
all of the hexagon's interior.

The historical case files and exact cubic capacity remain available as
compatibility or alternative derivations. No certificate blob, selector,
singleton gap, or CE1 conditional hypothesis is removed by this compression.
