# Trace-Exact AB Envelopes

Status: Proven

This note refines the ordinary AB-set by conditioning the **source triangles**
on an actual maximal boundary endpoint.  It is the correct local object for
visualizing and using a boundary gap.  It is not obtained by cutting the
ordinary AB-set by an affine half-plane.

All indices are modulo $6$.  Put

$$
X_i(t)=V_i+t(V_{i+1}-V_i),\qquad 0\le t\le1,
$$

and let $\mathcal C_i$ be the closed $120^\circ$ cone at $V_i$ generated by
$V_{i-1}-V_i$ and $V_{i+1}-V_i$.  For a closed unit equilateral triangle $S$
with $V_i\in\operatorname{int}S$, define its actual corner reaches

$$
\begin{aligned}
A_i(S)&=\max\{t\in[0,1]:V_i+t(V_{i-1}-V_i)\in S\},\\
B_i(S)&=\max\{t\in[0,1]:X_i(t)\in S\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
$$

The maxima exist because $S$ is closed.  Convexity and
$V_i\in\operatorname{int}S$ give the exact closed traces

$$
S\cap[V_i,V_{i-1}]
=\{V_i+t(V_{i-1}-V_i):0\le t\le A_i(S)\},
$$

$$
S\cap[V_i,V_{i+1}]
=X_i([0,B_i(S)]).
\tag{2}
$$

## 1. Endpoint-conditioned source families

For lower boundary demands $a,b\in[0,1]$, define the **trace-exact AB
envelopes**

$$
\mathcal E_i^{\rightarrow}(a\mid b)
=
\bigcup\left\{
S\cap\mathcal C_i:
\begin{array}{l}
S\text{ is a closed unit equilateral triangle},\\
V_i\in\operatorname{int}S,\quad
A_i(S)\ge a,\quad B_i(S)=b
\end{array}
\right\},
\tag{3}
$$

$$
\mathcal E_i^{\leftarrow}(a\mid b)
=
\bigcup\left\{
S\cap\mathcal C_i:
\begin{array}{l}
S\text{ is a closed unit equilateral triangle},\\
V_i\in\operatorname{int}S,\quad
A_i(S)=a,\quad B_i(S)\ge b
\end{array}
\right\},
\tag{4}
$$

and the two-sided fiber

$$
\mathcal E_i^{=}(a,b)
=
\bigcup\left\{
S\cap\mathcal C_i:
V_i\in\operatorname{int}S,
\ A_i(S)=a,
\ B_i(S)=b
\right\}.
\tag{5}
$$

The arrow indicates which adjacent trace has been fixed exactly: in
$\mathcal E_i^{\rightarrow}(a\mid b)$ the forward reach is exactly $b$; in
$\mathcal E_i^{\leftarrow}(a\mid b)$ the backward reach is exactly $a$.

The ordinary AB-set at $V_i$ is denoted by $\mathcal K_i(a,b)$: it is the
union in the corner cone of all closed unit equilateral triangles containing
$V_i$ and the two boundary points at reaches $a,b$.  This is the rotated copy
of the set $K(a,b)$ in [`20090`](20090_ab_set_index.md).

## 2. Basic properties

### Theorem 2.1 (trace-exact subunion)

For every $a,b\in[0,1]$,

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E_i^{\rightarrow}(a\mid b),\quad
\mathcal E_i^{\leftarrow}(a\mid b),\quad
\mathcal E_i^{=}(a,b)
\subseteq
\mathcal K_i(a,b).
}
\tag{6}
$$

If the corresponding source family is nonempty, then its exact edge sections
are

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E_i^{\rightarrow}(a\mid b)\cap e_{i,i+1}
=X_i([0,b]),
}
\tag{7}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E_i^{\leftarrow}(a\mid b)\cap e_{i-1,i}
=\{V_i+t(V_{i-1}-V_i):0\le t\le a\}.
}
\tag{8}
$$

The two-sided envelope has both exact sections simultaneously.

### Proof

Every source triangle in (3) contains the boundary point at backward reach
$a$ because $A_i(S)\ge a$, and it contains the point at forward reach $b$
because $B_i(S)=b$.  Hence its corner-cone portion belongs to the ordinary
AB-union $\mathcal K_i(a,b)$.  This proves the first inclusion in (6).  The
second and third are identical.

For every source triangle in (3), equation (2) gives the forward closed trace
$X_i([0,b])$.  Therefore the union of all such traces is exactly
$X_i([0,b])$ whenever the family is nonempty.  This proves (7).  Equation (8)
and the two-sided assertion follow by reflection.  $\square$

### Theorem 2.2 (actual-role inclusion)

Let $T_i=\overline{U_i}$ be an actual closed V role with actual maximal
reaches $(A_i,B_i)$.  Then

$$
\boxed{
T_i\cap\mathcal C_i
\subseteq
\mathcal E_i^{\rightarrow}(a\mid B_i)
\qquad(0\le a\le A_i),
}
\tag{9}
$$

$$
\boxed{
T_i\cap\mathcal C_i
\subseteq
\mathcal E_i^{\leftarrow}(A_i\mid b)
\qquad(0\le b\le B_i),
}
\tag{10}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{
T_i\cap\mathcal C_i
\subseteq
\mathcal E_i^{=}(A_i,B_i).
}
\tag{11}
$$

### Proof

The triangle $T_i$ itself is one of the source triangles in every displayed
union: it has $V_i$ in its interior, the declared actual reaches, and it
realizes each selected lower bound.  $\square$

## 3. Exact representation of an actual V-gap

Suppose the actual V-gap on $e_{i,i+1}$ is

$$
J_i=X_i([\ell,r]).
$$

By the definition of actual maximal reaches,

$$
\boxed{
\ell=B_i,
\qquad
r=1-A_{i+1}.
}
\tag{12}
$$

Consequently the incident open traces are exactly

$$
U_i\cap e_{i,i+1}=X_i([0,\ell)),
$$

$$
U_{i+1}\cap e_{i,i+1}=X_i((r,1]).
\tag{13}
$$

For any selected lower bounds $a\le A_i$ and $b\le B_{i+1}$, Theorems 2.1
and 2.2 give the proof-valid local overapproximations

$$
T_i\cap\mathcal C_i
\subseteq
\mathcal E_i^{\rightarrow}(a\mid\ell),
$$

$$
T_{i+1}\cap\mathcal C_{i+1}
\subseteq
\mathcal E_{i+1}^{\leftarrow}(1-r\mid b).
\tag{14}
$$

On the common edge their closed sections are precisely

$$
X_i([0,\ell])
\quad\text{and}\quad
X_i([r,1]).
\tag{15}
$$

The endpoints belong to the closures but not to the original open roles.
A nonincident V role cannot contain a relative-interior point of the edge by
the distance-one obstruction.  Hence, under a hypothetical cover,

$$
\boxed{J_i=X_i([\ell,r])\subset U_C.}
\tag{16}
$$

This includes the singleton case $\ell=r$.

## 4. Relation with the existing radial capacities

The exact-trace families are smaller than the ordinary AB-union, not larger.
Therefore every upper bound proved for the ordinary union remains a valid
upper bound for a trace-exact envelope.  In particular:

- the own-ray coordinate is at most $c_{\max}(a,b)$;
- the permitted forward neighboring-ray coordinate is at most $C_+(a,b)$;
- the reflected coordinate is at most $C_-(a,b)$.

Thus the type-aware radial witnesses in
[`2608`](../../26XX_enclosing_triangle_tools/2608_residual_hull_finite_enclosure_principle.md)
remain unchanged.  Exact-trace conditioning repairs the boundary geometry and
improves visualization without weakening any radial exclusion.

## 5. Why this is not planar truncation

It is generally false that $\mathcal E_i^{\rightarrow}(a\mid b)$ is obtained
by intersecting $\mathcal K_i(a,b)$ with a half-plane through the endpoint
$X_i(b)$.  A source triangle whose edge trace stops exactly at $X_i(b)$ may
cross such a line away from the edge.  A planar clipping can therefore delete
part of the actual triangle and is not a safe role overapproximation.

The valid operation is the source restriction in (3)--(5): retain only those
unit equilateral source triangles whose **actual maximal trace** has the
prescribed endpoint, and then take their union.  For numerical figures one may
sample this source family.  Such sampled pictures are explanatory only; the
proof uses the exact set-theoretic definitions above and the established
ordinary AB capacity bounds.
