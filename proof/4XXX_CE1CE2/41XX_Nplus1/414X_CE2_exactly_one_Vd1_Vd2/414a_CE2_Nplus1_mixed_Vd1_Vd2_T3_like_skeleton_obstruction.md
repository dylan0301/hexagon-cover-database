# CE1/CE2, $N_+=1$, Mixed Vd1/Vd2--Positive-Support Obstruction

Status: Proven

This historical path is a compatibility wrapper.

## Theorem

Assume $N_+=1$, exactly one V role is Vd1 or Vd2, and at least one additional
V role has positive support on an adjacent radial arm. Then the full skeleton
cannot be covered.

## Proof

The Vd role and the additional positive-support role are two distinct members
of the $N_{\rm sp}$ class, and neither is supercritical. Thus

$$
N_++N_{\rm sp}\ge1+2=3.
$$
