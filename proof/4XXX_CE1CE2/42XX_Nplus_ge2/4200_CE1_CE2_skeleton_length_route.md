# CE1/CE2, $N_+\ge2$, Skeleton-Length Obstruction

Status: Proven

This historical path is a compatibility wrapper.

## Theorem

Every CE1/CE2 state with $N_+\ge2$ fails to cover the full hexagon skeleton.

## Proof

Normalize the unique C-role midpoint to $M_0$ and choose a supercritical
index $s\ne0$. The supercritical role and the C role both miss $M_s$.
Diameter locality forces an adjacent open V role to rescue $M_s$, and openness
makes that rescuer a positive-support role distinct from the supercritical
roles. Hence

$$
N_++N_{\rm sp}\ge2+1=3.
$$
